## MONEYOS — #1

### Killer loop

Connect accounts/data → understand cash position → detect waste/risk → forecast → recommend next action → user approval → execute or hand off.

### MVP

- recurring income/expense model
- bill and subscription detection
- cash-flow forecast
- savings opportunities
- debt payoff scenarios
- financial calendar
- natural-language finance queries
- approval-gated actions
- audit log

### Why now

Global mobile spending is shifting toward monetizable non-game services. Sensor Tower reports $167B global IAP in 2025, with non-game IAP surpassing games and growing 21% YoY. citeturn389268search6turn389268search3

Finance also has a strong willingness-to-pay profile when the application saves or protects money; MONEYOS therefore targets measurable financial outcomes rather than generic chat.

## Product principles

1. Never present fabricated financial certainty.
2. Separate observation, recommendation and execution.
3. Require confirmation for money movement or consequential actions.
4. Make every automated decision inspectable.
5. Minimize permissions and sensitive-data retention.
6. Route low-risk inference locally where possible.

## Subscription strategy

Baseline test: €9.99–€14.99/month and discounted annual plan, with a higher family/pro tier. Pricing must be validated against AI inference cost, retention and willingness-to-pay rather than copied from competitors.
