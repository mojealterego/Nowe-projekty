# PRD — Cashflow Collections Agent

## MVP
- invoice ingestion;
- aging buckets;
- customer payment-history model;
- collection priority score;
- next-action recommendation;
- message generation;
- approval/send adapter;
- outcome and promise-to-pay tracking.

## Guardrails
No threats, deceptive claims or autonomous legal escalation. Respect customer communication policies, opt-outs and quiet hours.

## KPIs
DSO reduction; overdue balance reduction; promise-to-pay conversion; human override rate; false-positive escalation rate.
