# PRD — Contract Obligation Agent

## MVP
- PDF/DOCX/email ingestion;
- clause extraction with citations;
- normalized obligation schema;
- owner and system assignment;
- deadline scheduler;
- renewal and SLA alerts;
- evidence checklist;
- review workflow.

## Safety
Every extracted obligation retains source span and confidence. Low-confidence clauses require human confirmation before creating consequential reminders or escalations.

## KPIs
Extraction precision >95% on benchmark; missed-deadline incidents reduced; reviewer time per contract reduced by >60%.
