# PRD — Procurement Scout

## MVP
- purchase request intake;
- supplier discovery adapters;
- quote/document parser;
- normalized offer schema;
- TCO calculator;
- supplier risk flags;
- recommendation report;
- approval gate and audit trail.

## Guardrails
No autonomous purchase, payment or contract acceptance. Every external commitment requires explicit approval.

## KPIs
Time-to-shortlist <30 min; quote normalization >95%; savings opportunity surfaced per sourcing event; human approval rate >99% for committed actions.
